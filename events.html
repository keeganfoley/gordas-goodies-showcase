How It Works

Planning your order is easy

1. Reach out

Send us your event date, headcount, and any flavor requests by phone, email, or Instagram DM. We ask for at least 48 hours' advance notice.

2. We'll build your order

We'll talk through quantities, packaging, and pricing so it fits your event and budget.

3. Pickup or delivery

Arrange pickup, or ask about delivery for events in the Springfield / Northern Virginia area.
