Our Story

A family recipe, shared one box at a time.

Gorda's Goodies started at home, the way most good recipes do — a family love of Peruvian alfajores turned into something we could share with our neighbors. Every cookie is still made by hand, filled with real dulce de leche, and packed with the same care we'd serve for our own family gatherings.

Today, we bake for everyday treats, holidays, and special events.
